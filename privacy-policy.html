Microcosm - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Overview

Microcosm ("the App") is a free-to-play idle game made by Sycamore Hill Studios ("we", "us"). This page says what data the App handles and why.

No accounts or servers of our own

The App has no sign-up of its own, and we don't run a server that receives your data. Your game progress (resources, organelles, upgrades and division data) is saved on your device.

Google Play Games

The App contains Google Play Games code for sign-in, achievements, leaderboards and cloud save, but it isn't connected to Play Games in the current version, so nothing is sent to Play Games. If we turn it on, we will update this page first. Play Games data is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we never receive your email address.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The App only shows an ad when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching one. There are no banner ads and no interstitial ads.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob collects and shares data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud: your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, approximate location (worked out from your IP address), how you interact with ads and the App, and crash and diagnostic data. Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Purchases and Microcosm Premium (Google Play)

In-app purchases and the Microcosm Premium subscription are handled by Google Play. We receive a record that a purchase was made so the App can deliver it. We never see your payment details. You can cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App tells you about resources collected while you were away. These notifications are created on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

What we don't do

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. We don't use an analytics service of our own, and we don't sell personal data.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Your Choices

You can delete your local game data at any time with "Reset Game" in Settings, by clearing the App's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the App. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
